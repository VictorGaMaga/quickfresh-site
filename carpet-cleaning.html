FAQ

How long does carpet take to dry?

Most carpets dry in a few hours. Dry time varies with airflow and humidity.

Can you remove all stains?

We aim for the best improvement possible, but results vary by stain type, age, and fibre.

Is pet urine/odour treatment extra?

Yes, heavy odour or urine treatment is quoted based on severity and area.

Whats included in a standard clean?

Inspection, pre-treatment, hot-water extraction, and a professional rinse.

Do you clean wool carpets?

Yes, we assess fibre and use suitable methods for wool and delicate blends.

Do you provide end of lease receipts?

Yes, we can provide receipts for end of lease and property managers.
