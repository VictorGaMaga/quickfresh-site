How long does carpet take to dry?

Most carpets dry in a few hours. Dry time varies with airflow, humidity, and carpet type. Opening windows or using fans helps.

Can you remove all stains?

We aim for the best improvement possible, but results may vary depending on stain type, age, and fibre. Some stains may remain lightly visible.
