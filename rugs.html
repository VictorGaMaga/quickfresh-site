Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about quotes, final pricing, drying times, and Perth metro service.

How fast can I get a quote?

Message us on WhatsApp or SMS, or use the email form on this page. Most customers get a fast quote range, then we confirm the final price before we start.

Why is the final price confirmed on-site?

Online and message quotes are based on the details shared. We do a quick on-site check so your final price is accurate and fair before any work begins.

Do you have a minimum call-out?

Yes. A $129 minimum call-out applies. Tile & grout cleaning has a $200 minimum. See terms & conditions.

How long does rug cleaning take to dry?

Most rugs dry in a few hours. Drying time depends on airflow, weather, fibre type, and soil level. Opening windows or using fans helps speed things up.

Can you guarantee stain or odour removal?

We always aim for the best possible improvement. Some stains or odours can be permanent, especially if they are older or have soaked deep.

Do I need to move furniture before you arrive?

Please remove small items and keep access clear for the areas being cleaned. If you are unsure about larger furniture, message us and we'll advise before your appointment.

Can I book multiple services in one visit?

Yes. You can often combine carpets, rugs, upholstery, mattresses, and tile & grout in one visit, depending on scope and timing.

Do you service my suburb?

We service Perth metro suburbs. Send your suburb and we'll quickly confirm availability.
