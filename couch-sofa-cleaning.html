FAQ

How long does a couch/sofa take to dry?

Most synthetic fabrics dry in ~6 hours with good airflow (fans/windows). For best results, allow up to 24 hours before regular use.

Can you remove all stains?

We can improve most stains, but some can be permanent (dye loss/bleaching/old set stains). Well explain whats realistic after inspection.

Is pet urine/odour treatment extra?

Yes. It can be done, but upholstery urine/odour is more complex than carpets. We assess on-site and quote based on severity and affected areas.

Do you clean fabric sofas?

Yes we mainly clean synthetic upholstery (polyester/microfibre). Delicate fabrics are assessed first and cleaned with suitable methods.

Do you offer fabric protection?

Yes we can apply a fabric protector (Fabricoat) to help resist staining and slow spill absorption. It dries quickly and helps keep fabric looking better between cleans. It doesnt replace professional cleaning.
