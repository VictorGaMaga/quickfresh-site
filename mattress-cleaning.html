FAQ

How long does a mattress take to dry?

Most mattresses feel dry in 6-10 hours with good airflow (fans/windows). For best results, allow up to 24 hours before making the bed as normal.

Can you remove all stains?

We can improve most stains, but some can be permanent (bleaching/dye loss/old set stains). We'll explain whats realistic after inspection.

Is urine/odour treatment extra?

Yes. It can be done, but it depends on severity and affected areas. We assess on-site and quote accordingly.

Do you clean mattresses on-site?

Yes cleaning is done on-site in your home. If a mattress is too fragile or has structural issues, we'll advise the safest option after inspection.

Do you offer mattress protection?

Optional fabric protection can help reduce staining and slow spill absorption. Its not a guarantee and doesnt replace professional cleaning.
